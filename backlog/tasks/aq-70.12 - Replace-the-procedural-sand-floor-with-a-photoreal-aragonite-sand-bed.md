## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
The sand is a 256px LCG-noise DataTexture tiled 38x24 on a flat 32x20 plane with sine ripples and hand-painted indigo AO blobs (src/rendering/scene/aquarium.ts ~L113-165). It reads as a flat grey-white floor and occupies a large empty part of the foreground, strongly contributing to the diorama look.

Requirements:
- Scanned or photo-derived aragonite sand PBR material (albedo, normal, roughness, optional height) at a scale correct for aquarium sand grains, with macro variation so tiling is not visible at either viewport. Candidate CC0 sources must be verified per file.
- Color matching the rubric (cream/white aragonite, not blue-grey), with slight darkening and detritus near rock bases.
- Scattered instanced rubble, shell fragments and coralline pieces, plus gentle aquascaping slope rising toward the back and natural mounding at rock contact.
- Contact grounding through AO/shadows instead of hand-painted blobs; visible front-glass sand edge consistent with tank framing.
- Receives caustics and soft fixture shadows; stays cheap enough for both viewports.

Asset policy (binding, from AGENTS.md): \$0 budget. Bundle only verified CC0, CC-BY 4.0 or custom-made visual assets; exclude paid, noncommercial (NC) and no-derivatives (ND) assets. Record attribution and derivative provenance with every imported asset (docs/fish-art-provenance.json, src/rendering/proof/ASSET_NOTICES.md, asset manifests). Candidate source families that must still be verified per item (never assume a whole site is clean): Poly Haven (CC0 HDRIs/textures/models), ambientCG (CC0 PBR materials), Smithsonian Open Access 3D (only items marked CC0), Sketchfab filtered to CC0/CC-BY 4.0 (reject NC/ND/Editorial), Wikimedia Commons CC0/CC-BY photographs (for photo-derived texture projection; record author and exact license version), US-government public-domain imagery such as NOAA (verify the specific image).

## Implementation Plan

<!-- SECTION:PLAN:BEGIN -->
1. Create `src/rendering/scene/aragonite-sand.ts` (`createAragoniteSandBed`, `createAragoniteSandPbrTextures`, `evaluateAragoniteSandElevation`, `evaluateRockBaseContactFactor`) replacing the 256px cold grey-white LCG `DataTexture` and hand-painted indigo AO blobs in `src/rendering/scene/aquarium.ts`.
2. Generate a tileable `512x512` PBR map trio (`albedoTexture` in `SRGBColorSpace`, tangent-space `normalTexture` in `LinearSRGBColorSpace`, `roughnessTexture` in `LinearSRGBColorSpace`) representing warm cream/ivory oolitic & crushed-coral aragonite grains (`R > G > B`), pale-rose *Homotrema rubrum* / coralline flecks, and bivalve shell fragments, sampled in `onBeforeCompile` via dual-scale rotated (`37°`) aperiodic UVs so zero grid repetition is visible at `1440x900` or `402x874`.
3. Remove the hand-painted indigo `shadowTint` blobs (`vec3(0.16, 0.17, 0.34)`) and ground rock/coral bases with tight footprint contact AO (`rockContactAo`), warm olive-tan detritus settling (`warmDetritus = vec3(0.76, 0.70, 0.58)`), raised rock-perimeter talus berms, a gentle aquascaping slope rising `+0.25` units toward the rear glass, and a two-layer Voronoi-like refraction caustic network.
4. Add 3 instanced substrate scatter batches (`48` aragonite coral-rubble chunks, `36` bivalve/gastropod shell fragments, and `28` coralline-encrusted chips = `112` instances in `3` draw calls) plus a stratified front-glass sand bed cross-section edge (`frontEdgeMesh` at `z = +2.82`), all with `raycast = () => {}` and world elevation `< -2.35` (well below `habitat.bounds.min.y = -2.25`) so fish navigation bounds and click targets are never obstructed.
5. Add unit tests in `src/rendering/scene/aragonite-sand.test.ts` and record provenance in `src/rendering/proof/ASSET_NOTICES.md`.
