## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
After the front-on reef scene, selected fish assets, and lifelike fish locomotion are established, add restrained motion to the aquarium habitat itself. This deferred refinement should make water, light, and soft reef organisms feel natural while keeping the tank sharp and the fish the main focus.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [x] #1 Subtle water-surface ripples make the tank feel alive without pulling attention from fish or service links.
- [x] #2 Soft, physically coherent caustic light shifts gently across sand, live rock, and coral; it does not flicker or use a sweeping background pan.
- [x] #3 Soft polyps and anemones respond gently to water movement, while rigid reef rock and hard coral structures stay stable.
- [x] #4 The front-on tank perspective and crisp deep focus remain intact; no blur, dark overlay, or moving background is used as a shortcut.
- [x] #5 Ambient habitat motion pauses with the aquarium controls, respects reduced-motion settings, and does not interfere with fish labels or link selection.
- [ ] #6 The added effects remain responsive at 402x874 CSS pixels for the iPhone Pro proxy and 1440x900 for a normal laptop; broader viewport matrices are out of scope.
<!-- AC:END -->

## Implementation Plan

<!-- SECTION:PLAN:BEGIN -->
### 1. Current Baseline Deficits in `src/rendering/scene/aquarium.ts` (Lines 37–47)
- **Hard, unmodulated white directional lights only**: Line 39 currently creates two bare `THREE.DirectionalLight(0xffffff, 3)` at `(1, 6, 5)` and `THREE.DirectionalLight(0xffffff, 1)` at `(-4, 2, 3)` with no `HemisphereLight` floor bounce, no actinic blue (`#4f9cff`) reef fill, and no caustic shimmer modulation.
- **No top water-surface meniscus or ripple plane**: Lines 43–47 only instantiate `sand` (`y = -2.8`) and `rear` (`z = -5.5`); there is no upper water-surface mesh near `y = +2.75` to provide subtle surface ripples.
- **No per-frame caustic or soft-coral uniform updates wired into `render()`**: Although `src/rendering/scene/underwater-optics.ts` and `src/rendering/scene/coral-material.ts` provide tested controllers and vertex-mask shaders, `createAquarium()` in `src/rendering/scene/aquarium.ts` does not yet invoke them during the animation loop.

### 2. Step-by-Step Changes Required in `src/rendering/scene/aquarium.ts`
1. **Replace the 2-light white rig (line 39) with a 3-tier reef lighting rig**:
   - **Key Overhead Daylight (`DirectionalLight`)**: Color `#f4f9ff` (crisp reef daylight), base intensity `2.8`, positioned at `(1.2, 6.5, 4.5)`.
   - **Actinic Reef Fill (`DirectionalLight`)**: Color `#4f9cff` (royal-blue actinic fill), base intensity `1.15`, positioned at `(-4.0, 2.5, 3.0)` to rim-light fish silhouettes and accentuate coral growth-tip highlights (`COLOR_0.g`).
   - **Sand-Bed Bounce (`HemisphereLight`)**: Sky color `#7dd3fc` (upper water column) and ground color `#d6cbb5` (warm aragonite sand bounce) at intensity `0.65` so rock overhangs, pass-through caves, and fish ventral bellies have soft warm fill rather than harsh black shadows.
2. **Wire `createRestrainedCausticLightController` & Upward-Surface Caustic Projection (AC #2, #5)**:
   - Instantiate `const causticController = createRestrainedCausticLightController(keyLight, fillLight)` from `src/rendering/scene/underwater-optics.ts`.
   - Call `causticController.update(elapsedSec, isPaused(), reducedMotion.matches)` inside `render()` in `src/rendering/scene/aquarium.ts`.
   - Inject upward-facing (`max(0.0, normal.y)`) dual-wave caustic modulation (`0.22 Hz` and `0.37 Hz`, matching `evaluateCausticIntensity` in `src/rendering/scene/underwater-optics.ts`) onto `sand.material` and live-rock/coral materials so soft caustic bands shift gently across horizontal sand ledges, live rock crests, and coral plates without flickering or panning the background.
   - Freeze the caustic time uniform at `0` whenever `isPaused()` or `reducedMotion.matches` is true.
3. **Add a Restrained Top Water-Surface Ripple Plane (AC #1, #5)**:
   - Add a subdivided upper water-surface plane (`new THREE.PlaneGeometry(24, 10, 48, 16)`) at `y = 2.72`, `rotation.x = Math.PI / 2`, with a subtle translucent `MeshPhysicalMaterial` / `MeshStandardMaterial` (`color: '#5ec8e5'`, `transparent: true`, `opacity: 0.22`, `roughness: 0.15`).
   - Displace vertex `y` offsets in `render()` (or vertex shader) using `evaluateSurfaceRippleOffset(x, z, elapsedSec, { paused: isPaused(), reducedMotion: reducedMotion.matches })` from `src/rendering/scene/underwater-optics.ts` (`amplitudeM = 0.008 m`).
   - Override `waterSurface.raycast = () => {}` so the surface mesh can never intercept pointer or click raycasts intended for service fish.
4. **Connect Soft-Polyp & Anemone `COLOR_0.r` Sway while Keeping Hard Skeletons Rigid (AC #3, #5)**:
   - When mounting modular corals via `mountCoralsOnSockets()` (`src/rendering/scene/reef-assembly.ts`) and `applyCoralRawMaskMaterial()` (`src/rendering/scene/coral-material.ts`), update each returned `CoralShaderController.update(elapsedSec, isPaused(), reducedMotion.matches)` inside `render()`.
   - Because `applyCoralRawMaskMaterial` reads raw `COLOR_0.r` (`flexibility`), calcified hard-coral skeletons (`Acropora`, `Montipora`, `Favia`, live rock, where `COLOR_0.r == 0`) remain 100% rigid (`0.0 m` displacement), while fleshy LPS vesicles (`Euphyllia`), anemone tentacles (`Entacmaea`), and octocoral fronds (`Sinularia`, `Sarcophyton`, `Zoanthus`) sway gently (`1.5–12 mm`) and freeze at `t = 0` when paused or under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
   - Per `docs/open-source-asset-and-motion-research.md` §6.3 (Malul et al. 2020), treat per-archetype frequencies and amplitudes as artistic parameters bounded to subtle aquarium flow rather than universal biological constants.
5. **Responsive & Accessibility Verification (AC #4, #5, #6)**:
   - Verify at both `1440x900` (desktop) and `402x874` (iPhone Pro proxy) that crisp deep focus is preserved (no blur, vignette, or dark overlay), pause and `prefers-reduced-motion` freeze all three motion channels (ripples, caustics, soft polyps), and fish label/link selection remains unobstructed.

Root visual refinement 2026-11-11: reduce the oversized, bright surface band and caustic shadow contrast shown in exact-size native browser review. Preserve the shared frozen clock, geometry, assets and interactions. Verify actual WebGL output and pause at 1440x900 and 402x874; do not claim final art or frame-budget acceptance from unit tests.
