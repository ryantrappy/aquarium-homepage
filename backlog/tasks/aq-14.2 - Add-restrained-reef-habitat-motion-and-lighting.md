## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
After the front-on reef scene, selected fish assets, and lifelike fish locomotion are established, add restrained motion to the aquarium habitat itself. This deferred refinement should make water, light, and soft reef organisms feel natural while keeping the tank sharp and the fish the main focus.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [ ] #1 Subtle water-surface ripples make the tank feel alive without pulling attention from fish or service links.
- [ ] #2 Soft, physically coherent caustic light shifts gently across sand, live rock, and coral; it does not flicker or use a sweeping background pan.
- [ ] #3 Soft polyps and anemones respond gently to water movement, while rigid reef rock and hard coral structures stay stable.
- [ ] #4 The front-on tank perspective and crisp deep focus remain intact; no blur, dark overlay, or moving background is used as a shortcut.
- [ ] #5 Ambient habitat motion pauses with the aquarium controls, respects reduced-motion settings, and does not interfere with fish labels or link selection.
- [ ] #6 The added effects remain responsive at 402x874 CSS pixels for the iPhone Pro proxy and 1440x900 for a normal laptop; broader viewport matrices are out of scope.
<!-- AC:END -->
