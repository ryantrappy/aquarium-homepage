## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
After the starter three establish an accepted photoreal pipeline and material contract, the rest of the roster must reach the same standard so any service can be assigned any fish without a realism drop. Current assets for these species are procedural builds (royal gramma, Banggai cardinalfish, mandarin dragonet) or unreviewed splat-fish candidates (chromis, firefish, watchman goby, tailspot blenny, six-line wrasse, McCosker's flasher wrasse, lyretail anthias, flame angelfish, copperband butterflyfish).

Species-specific realism notes to verify against references:
- Royal gramma: magenta-to-yellow gradient with soft transition, black dorsal eyespot, thin face lines.
- Banggai cardinalfish: silver body with three black bars, white pearl spotting, very long first dorsal and anal fins, translucent fins.
- Mandarin dragonet: intricate blue/orange maze pattern (high-resolution albedo required), scaleless mucus-coated skin with strong wet specular.
- Blue-green chromis: iridescent blue-green guanine sheen (iridescence map), forked caudal.
- Firefish: elongated body, very tall first dorsal spine, white-to-red/purple gradient.
- Yellow watchman goby: yellow body with faint blue spots and bands, bottom-dwelling posture, large eyes.
- Tailspot blenny: cirri on head, black tail spot, elongated blenny shape.
- Six-line wrasse: thin horizontal blue/orange stripes, green-and-white tail markings.
- McCosker's flasher wrasse: male display fins with long dorsal filaments, red/blue/yellow coloring.
- Lyretail anthias: lunate lyre caudal with filaments, orange/pink female or magenta male coloring (match AQ-60 female scale decision).
- Flame angelfish: red-orange with vertical black bars, blue fin edging.
- Copperband butterflyfish: copper bands with dark margins, long tubular snout, dorsal eyespot.

Asset policy (binding, from AGENTS.md): \$0 budget. Bundle only verified CC0, CC-BY 4.0 or custom-made visual assets; exclude paid, noncommercial (NC) and no-derivatives (ND) assets. Record attribution and derivative provenance with every imported asset (docs/fish-art-provenance.json, src/rendering/proof/ASSET_NOTICES.md, asset manifests). Candidate source families that must still be verified per item (never assume a whole site is clean): Poly Haven (CC0 HDRIs/textures/models), ambientCG (CC0 PBR materials), Smithsonian Open Access 3D (only items marked CC0), Sketchfab filtered to CC0/CC-BY 4.0 (reject NC/ND/Editorial), Wikimedia Commons CC0/CC-BY photographs (for photo-derived texture projection; record author and exact license version), US-government public-domain imagery such as NOAA (verify the specific image).
