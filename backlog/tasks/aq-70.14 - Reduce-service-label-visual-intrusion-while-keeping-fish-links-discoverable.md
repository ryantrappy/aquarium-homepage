## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
In the baseline screenshot, always-on dark pill labels float over the reef and read as a game HUD, which undermines the realistic tank even when assets improve. Fish are the service links, so discoverability and accessibility must be preserved (AQ-12 AC #4, AQ-50, AQ-63).

This needs a user decision before implementation. Options to present:
1. Labels hidden by default; shown on pointer hover/near-pointer, keyboard focus and touch-and-hold, with a brief reveal at first load.
2. Always visible but restyled as a small, low-contrast etched-glass caption with reduced opacity that brightens on hover/focus.
3. Always visible on touch devices (no hover) and hover/focus-revealed on pointer devices.
Accessibility: fish remain native links with accessible names regardless of visual label state; the Services modal remains the complete list.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->
