## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
The eight procedural coral colonies (src/rendering/modeling/build_*.py, packaged in src/rendering/assets/corals/) are a large part of the cartoon read: uniform saturated pastel hues, repeated identical branch instances, cotton-swab Acropora tips, lollipop Euphyllia, and an emissive neon brain coral. AQ-42 tracks coral asset authoring generally; this task sets the photoreal bar and specific fixes.

Per-archetype requirements (verify with rubric references):
- Acropora staghorn/table: tapering branches with visible radial corallites and a lighter axial corallite at each tip; natural branching irregularity; colors mostly tan/brown/green/blue-purple with lighter growth tips rather than uniform pastel.
- Montipora plate: whorled, thin plates with fine polyp pores and a lighter growing rim; slight undulation.
- Euphyllia torch/hammer: long fleshy, translucent tentacles with distinct tips (torch: rounded tips; hammer: anchor/T-shaped tips), visible skeleton walls at the base.
- Brain coral (Diploria-type): meandering valleys and ridges with correct proportions, muted greens/browns with fluorescent valleys via a fluorescence mask, not a blanket emissive.
- Bubble-tip anemone: bulb-tipped tentacles, translucent tissue, column hidden in rock crevice.
- Zoanthids: individual polyps with patterned oral discs and short tentacles, colony spread with size variation.
- All: photo-derived or reference-accurate albedo, normal/roughness/AO maps, fluorescence mask channel for the LED/fluorescence task, thickness or transmission data for soft tissue, multiple variants per archetype with within-species color/shape variation to break repetition, and preserved contracts: COLOR_0 flex/tip/phase/contactAO masks (AQ-53), socket bases (AQ-52) and manifest hashes (AQ-42.1).

Asset policy (binding, from AGENTS.md): \$0 budget. Bundle only verified CC0, CC-BY 4.0 or custom-made visual assets; exclude paid, noncommercial (NC) and no-derivatives (ND) assets. Record attribution and derivative provenance with every imported asset (docs/fish-art-provenance.json, src/rendering/proof/ASSET_NOTICES.md, asset manifests). Candidate source families that must still be verified per item (never assume a whole site is clean): Poly Haven (CC0 HDRIs/textures/models), ambientCG (CC0 PBR materials), Smithsonian Open Access 3D (only items marked CC0), Sketchfab filtered to CC0/CC-BY 4.0 (reject NC/ND/Editorial), Wikimedia Commons CC0/CC-BY photographs (for photo-derived texture projection; record author and exact license version), US-government public-domain imagery such as NOAA (verify the specific image).
