## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Real photographs of reef tanks show strong contact darkening in crevices, under coral bases and where fish pass near rock. The current scene fakes this with hand-painted indigo AO blobs in the sand shader (aquarium.ts ~L151-157) and baked COLOR_0.a, so contact reads as painted. Thin Acropora branches shimmer, and fluorescent tissue cannot glow selectively.

Requirements:
- Screen-space ambient occlusion (for example three's GTAOPass, or an N8AO-style pass; verify license) tuned for small-scale crevices without halos around fish.
- Anti-aliasing suited to thin geometry (SMAA, or TAA if motion stability allows).
- Selective bloom limited to fluorescence/emissive masks; no general glow.
- Optional subtle color grade (LUT) matched to the rubric.
- Forbidden by project policy: depth-of-field blur, vignette, dark scrim or blue veil. The tank must stay sharp with deep focus.
- Passes participate in the quality tiers from the budget task; label projection and hit-testing must not depend on post-processed buffers.
- Options to evaluate: three/examples/jsm EffectComposer + GTAOPass + SMAAPass + OutputPass; the pmndrs postprocessing library (check license); or three's WebGPURenderer with TSL post nodes (check Safari/iOS support).

Responsive verification uses only 1440x900 (laptop) and 402x874 CSS pixels at scale 1 (iPhone 18 Pro proxy); do not add a broader device matrix.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->
