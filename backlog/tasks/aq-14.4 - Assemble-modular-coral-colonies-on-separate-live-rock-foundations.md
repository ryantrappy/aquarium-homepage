## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Per user direction (2026-11-03), realistic coral requires modeling each coral piece separately on top of bare live rock rather than stretching the monolithic `candidate-reef.glb` scan (`scale: { x: 4.6, y: 6.2, z: 1.8 }`). Assemble the aquascape from two bare live-rock island foundations (`live-rock-island-left.glb`, `live-rock-island-right.glb`) with named surface-normal socket empties (`socket_*`) and mount modular, uniformly scaled (`[1, 1, 1]`) coral colonies across five biological archetypes as specified in `docs/modular-coral-and-live-rock-spec.md`.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [ ] #1 `rebuildHabitat()` in `src/rendering/scene/aquarium.ts` places bare live-rock islands at uniform `[1, 1, 1]` scale with two-tone carbonate (`#5A5246`, `roughness: 0.92`) and purple coralline algae (`#6B2D5C`, `roughness: 0.68`) shading, pass-through overhang caves, and grounded sand contact without non-uniform vertical stretching.
- [ ] #2 Separate coral colony GLBs (Branching *Acropora* SPS, Plating *Montipora* SPS, two-mesh *Euphyllia* Torch/Hammer LPS, mounded Brain/Cushion LPS, and recessed Bubble-Tip Anemone + *Zoanthid* mats) mount onto named `socket_*` nodes with flared encrusting basal skirts and baked `COLOR_0.a` contact ambient occlusion.
- [ ] #3 All coral meshes follow the standardized `COLOR_0` vertex attribute contract (`R = flexibilityMask`, `G = growthTipOrPolypMask`, `B = phaseSeed`, `A = contactAO`) so calcified stony skeletons and *Euphyllia* stalks stay 100% rigid (`R = 0.0`) while fleshy tentacles sway out of phase (`R = h^1.8`) and freeze when paused or under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- [ ] #4 `deriveReefHabitat()` regenerates `src/rendering/scene/reef-habitat.json` from the combined rock + stony coral geometry, binding `socket_anemone_crevice_01` to the Ocellaris Clownfish `anemone` tether, exposing upward-facing rock ledges as grazing/perching anchors, and registering all solid rock/coral meshes in `hitMeshes` for accurate fish label and click occlusion at `1440x900` and `402x874`.
<!-- AC:END -->
