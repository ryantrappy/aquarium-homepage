## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
"Realistic" has repeatedly been self-asserted by authoring notes (for example src/rendering/modeling/README.md calls procedural corals "finished") while the rendered result is cartoonish. Every later look-dev task needs an objective target and pass/fail rubric that reviewers can apply to captures.

Scope of the research:
- Real front-on home reef tanks (not open ocean, not blurry backgrounds) under typical 14,000-20,000 K LED fixtures with white + royal-blue/actinic channels.
- Per element, what makes it read as real: fish skin (wet specular, guanine iridescence where species-appropriate, countershading, soft pattern edges, scale and lateral-line micro-detail, translucent fin membranes with visible rays, glossy eye cornea); coral (corallite/polyp texture, color ranges per genus used here: Acropora staghorn/table, Montipora plate, Euphyllia torch/hammer, brain/Diploria-type, bubble-tip anemone, zoanthids; tissue translucency; localized fluorescence under actinic light); live rock (carbonate beige/tan/grey base with patchy purple/pink coralline algae, porosity, small encrusting life); sand (aragonite grain size, cream/white color, shell and rubble fragments, detritus); water (clarity at aquarium scale, caustic network frequency/contrast/speed, underside-of-surface reflection and Snell window, very slight blue shift only at the back); glass (edges, silicone seams, faint reflections).
- Quantitative targets where measurable from photographs: luminance histogram/contrast range, saturation distribution per element (compare against the baseline screenshot, whose coral saturation is visibly too uniform and high), dominant hue ranges, caustic spatial frequency.
- Shortlist of candidate licensed sources for fish, coral, rock, sand textures/scans and HDR environments, each with license verified individually.
- Input for the baked-lighting spike: does a real-time-only renderer realistically reach the rubric in a browser, or is baked/pre-rendered lighting needed for the static reef?

Reference images are research links only; do not bundle photographs unless the specific image is CC0/CC-BY 4.0 and attributed.

## Baseline audit (user screenshot 2026-11-05, saved at docs/asset-evidence/photorealism/baseline-2026-11-05-user-screenshot.jpg)

The user reports the scene looks cartoonish rather than hyper-realistic. Root inspected the screenshot and the current source. Findings:

**Fish**
- Regal blue tang (src/rendering/assets/fish/fish-blue-tang.glb, generated by src/rendering/modeling/build_blue_tang.py with mathematically generated 1024px base/normal/ORM textures) reads as a vector cartoon: flat saturated royal blue, hard-edged black "palette" band, no visible scale micro-detail, no wet specular highlight, opaque fins without rays or translucency, eye without a cornea highlight, overly smooth bulbous silhouette.
- Yellow tang and clownfish (src/rendering/proof/candidate-real-*.glb) look small and toy-like at runtime; the yellow tang seen near edge-on reads as a flat yellow disc with spiky fins.
- The 9 splat-fish GLBs (src/rendering/assets/splat-fish/) are unreviewed user candidates and must not be assumed acceptable.

**Coral**
- All 8 colonies are procedural Blender Python builds (src/rendering/modeling/build_*.py) shaded by vertex-color masks in src/rendering/scene/coral-material.ts. Hues are uniformly saturated pastels (lavender, cyan, mint, pink); identical branch instances repeat; Acropora tips read as cotton swabs; Euphyllia reads as lollipop clusters.
- Brain coral uses a generated emissive texture (build_brain_coral.py emits em_img) and reads as glowing neon-green plastic.
- No polyp-scale surface detail, no tissue translucency, no believable fluorescence (fluorescence should be localized and driven by actinic light, not a blanket emissive).

**Live rock** (already tracked by AQ-59): the runtime rock shader palette in coral-material.ts (cDeepShadow ... cCorPlumMagenta, roughly lines 498-513) is indigo/violet, so rock reads as blue foam instead of beige/tan carbonate with patchy purple/pink coralline algae.

**Sand**: a 256px LCG-noise DataTexture tiled 38x24 on a 32x20 plane with sine ripples (src/rendering/scene/aquarium.ts ~L113-165). It reads as a flat grey-white floor; the shader also paints hard-coded indigo AO blobs under the islands. Shadows on the sand are sharp single-sun silhouettes from one 1024px directional shadow map, unlike the soft multi-emitter shadows under reef LED fixtures.

**Water, background, glass**: rear plane is a shader gradient with sine "sunbeams" (aquarium.ts ~L167-206); scene.background is a solid #183654; linear Fog(18..36) has essentially no effect at tank distances (camera z=9); no glass panes, edges or reflections; the water surface is a 26%-opacity plane that produces a flat band at the top; no wavelength-dependent absorption.

**Lighting/renderer**: HemisphereLight + three DirectionalLights, no scene.environment / PMREM image-based lighting, so MeshStandardMaterial surfaces have no specular reflections and look matte or plastic. ACES tone mapping at exposure 1.02, no ambient-occlusion pass, no anti-aliasing beyond MSAA, no selective bloom for fluorescence, pixel ratio capped at 1.5, PCFSoftShadowMap deprecation warning in three r187. Caustics are sine-wave bands (underwater-optics.ts, coral-material.ts ~L370-375, sand shader) that read as soft stripes, not a caustic network.

**Composition**: two near-symmetric mounds on an empty sand plain; reef occupies only the lower half; large empty upper water and foreground; reads as a diorama rather than a stocked reef tank. Always-on dark pill labels read as a game HUD.

**Root causes (in priority order)**
1. Every visible asset is procedurally authored from math noise and primitive shapes instead of being derived from real organisms (photographs, scans or reference-accurate sculpting).
2. The renderer lacks image-based lighting, ambient occlusion, transmission/subsurface approximations and a real caustic/LED lighting model.
3. Scene composition, scale and tank framing do not match a real front-on reef tank.

Motion realism (body deformation, fin propulsion, turning) is tracked separately in AQ-12, AQ-49 and AQ-18..AQ-32; this initiative covers appearance (look-dev).

Asset policy (binding, from AGENTS.md): \$0 budget. Bundle only verified CC0, CC-BY 4.0 or custom-made visual assets; exclude paid, noncommercial (NC) and no-derivatives (ND) assets. Record attribution and derivative provenance with every imported asset (docs/fish-art-provenance.json, src/rendering/proof/ASSET_NOTICES.md, asset manifests). Candidate source families that must still be verified per item (never assume a whole site is clean): Poly Haven (CC0 HDRIs/textures/models), ambientCG (CC0 PBR materials), Smithsonian Open Access 3D (only items marked CC0), Sketchfab filtered to CC0/CC-BY 4.0 (reject NC/ND/Editorial), Wikimedia Commons CC0/CC-BY photographs (for photo-derived texture projection; record author and exact license version), US-government public-domain imagery such as NOAA (verify the specific image).
