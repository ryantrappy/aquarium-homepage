## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
These three species are the fish visible in the current scene and the most important realism failures. The regal blue tang (build_blue_tang.py, fish-blue-tang.glb) reads as a vector cartoon; the yellow tang and clownfish candidates (src/rendering/proof/candidate-real-*.glb) read as toys. Per AGENTS.md, visual modeling belongs to the user-managed Gemini agent; Codex agents must not create competing models.

Per-species realism requirements (verify against FishBase and the rubric reference photos):
- Regal blue tang (Paracanthurus hepatus): oval laterally compressed body, body depth roughly 40-45% of standard length (verify), black "palette" marking with organic soft edges and correct shape running from eye to caudal base, yellow caudal fin with black margins, yellow pectoral patch, subtle blue iridescent sheen, small terminal mouth, caudal spine detail.
- Yellow tang (Zebrasoma flavescens): near-disc, strongly compressed body, elongated snout, tall sail-like dorsal and anal fins, white caudal spine (scalpel), uniform lemon yellow with subtle tonal variation and faint horizontal lines, not flat yellow.
- Ocellaris clownfish (Amphiprion ocellaris): rounded fins, orange body with three white bands with thin black margins, rounded caudal, fleshy appearance; head band shape and fin margin widths matching references.
- All: photo-derived or reference-accurate hand-painted albedo (delit, no baked highlights), 2K body texture (or budget-defined), normal map with scale pattern and lateral line, roughness variation, separate fin parts with fin-ray geometry or masks, eye geometry with iris, pupil and cornea, maps and part names matching the fish material contract.
- Keep rig compatibility: joint names and tracks from AQ-49.1, archetypes from AQ-48, clean-pose clip player (AQ-58), swim-axis lengths in src/rendering/scene/fish-scale.ts and true relative sizes (AQ-60).
- Preserve existing candidates and the user's uncommitted splat-fish work; deliver new versions alongside, with provenance in docs/fish-art-provenance.json.

Asset policy (binding, from AGENTS.md): \$0 budget. Bundle only verified CC0, CC-BY 4.0 or custom-made visual assets; exclude paid, noncommercial (NC) and no-derivatives (ND) assets. Record attribution and derivative provenance with every imported asset (docs/fish-art-provenance.json, src/rendering/proof/ASSET_NOTICES.md, asset manifests). Candidate source families that must still be verified per item (never assume a whole site is clean): Poly Haven (CC0 HDRIs/textures/models), ambientCG (CC0 PBR materials), Smithsonian Open Access 3D (only items marked CC0), Sketchfab filtered to CC0/CC-BY 4.0 (reject NC/ND/Editorial), Wikimedia Commons CC0/CC-BY photographs (for photo-derived texture projection; record author and exact license version), US-government public-domain imagery such as NOAA (verify the specific image).
