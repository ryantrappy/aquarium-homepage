## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Even with photoreal assets, the current layout reads as a diorama: two near-symmetric mounds on an empty sand plain, reef only in the lower half of the frame, large empty foreground sand, and coral spaced like specimens. Scale between fish, coral branches and rock is not grounded in real units (AQ-58 notes the main scene still uses legacy world units).

Requirements:
- Define the virtual tank in real dimensions (for example a typical display tank around 120x60x60 cm; choose and document) with one metric scale shared by fish (AQ-60), corals and rock, and set camera FOV/position to match a viewer standing in front of the glass.
- Aquascape with asymmetric rock massing spanning most of the width, varied height, caves and channels, a back-rock layer for depth, dense coral coverage in natural growth zones (SPS high in light, LPS mid, zoanthids/anemones low or in crevices), and deliberate open swimming water so fish stay prominent.
- Framing that works for both 1440x900 landscape and 402x874 portrait without stretching (separate camera framing per aspect is acceptable).
- Regenerate habitat/navigation data (deriveReefHabitat, reef-habitat.json) and keep fish collision, anchors and hit-testing correct.
- Coordinate with AQ-14.4 (modular assembly), AQ-14.6 (background/tank environment) and AQ-62 (focal zoom).

Responsive verification uses only 1440x900 (laptop) and 402x874 CSS pixels at scale 1 (iPhone 18 Pro proxy); do not add a broader device matrix.
