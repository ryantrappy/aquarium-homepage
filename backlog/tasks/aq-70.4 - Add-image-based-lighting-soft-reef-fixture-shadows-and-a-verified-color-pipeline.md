## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
The scene has no scene.environment, so every MeshStandardMaterial lacks specular reflections and looks matte or plastic; this is one of the largest single contributors to the cartoon look. Shadows come from one 1024px directional light and read as sharp outdoor-sun silhouettes, whereas reef LED fixtures produce soft, multi-emitter penumbras. Tone mapping (ACES, exposure 1.02) has not been compared against AgX or Neutral, both available in three r187, and ACES can shift saturated blues.

Current setup: src/rendering/scene/aquarium.ts lines ~69-111 (renderer, HemisphereLight, key/fill/rim DirectionalLights, PCFSoftShadowMap which three r187 warns about).

Requirements and options to evaluate (implementer decides and documents):
- A reef-tank environment for PMREM: a custom-made equirectangular render of a tank interior with LED fixture emitters (for example rendered in Blender, which the pipeline already uses), or a procedurally built environment scene in the spirit of three's RoomEnvironment. Terrestrial CC0 HDRIs are generally wrong for underwater lighting but may help for glass reflections; verify license per file.
- Physically consistent light intensities and color temperatures aligned with the rubric.
- Soft shadows matching a wide overhead fixture (larger maps, VSM or PCSS-style filtering, or baked contact shadows from the baked-lighting spike), and removal of the deprecated shadow type warning.
- Color-space audit: albedo maps sRGB, normal/ORM/mask maps linear, COLOR_0 masks treated as data; add a test.
- Adaptive pixel ratio (up to 2 when budget allows) instead of the fixed 1.5 cap.

Responsive verification uses only 1440x900 (laptop) and 402x874 CSS pixels at scale 1 (iPhone 18 Pro proxy); do not add a broader device matrix.
