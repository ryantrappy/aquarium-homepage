This is the supported target roster for the reef aquarium, not a statement that all fish are implemented. The app currently has five catalog entries; the first five IDs below preserve those existing IDs. Treat each species as its own future animation task. Do not consider a still image sliding through the scene to be a finished fish animation.

## How to use these briefs

The habitat and appearance notes summarize the linked biological references. The final column is an animation design brief inferred from those observations, not a claim that a source measured a particular swimming speed or fin-beat rate. Implementers should make locomotion visibly distinct between species and animate body, fin, and tail motion as supported by the selected rendering approach. This roster does not recommend keeping these species together; it intentionally combines fish from different reef regions to serve one visual reef scene.

## Future freshwater biome

For the future freshwater catalog, select one biome: **Rio Negro blackwater flooded forest (igapó), focused on shallow flooded margins among submerged roots and branches**. This gives the separate freshwater task a specific habitat to research; no freshwater species roster is proposed here. Igapó refers to forest periodically flooded by acidic, dark water along the Rio Negro, and Rio Negro floodplain studies document fish use of inundated forest margins. [New York Botanical Garden](https://sweetgum.nybg.org/science/glossary/glossary-details/?irn=3293), [Frontiers in Ecology and Evolution](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/ecology-and-evolution/articles/10.3389/fevo.2021.602895/full)
